Privacy Policy for Wyvern Rush

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Wyvern Rush
Contact: wyvernrush@gmail.com
Android package: com.wyvernrush.game

This Privacy Policy explains how Wyvern Rush (“the App”) handles information when you use the game on Android. By installing or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

Wyvern Rush is a single-player mobile game. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your name, email address, or photos. Game progress is stored on your device. Advertising and (when enabled) in-app purchases are provided by Google and may process limited device and purchase-related data as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device, including:

  • Adventure progress (worlds and stages cleared)
  • High score / best run information
  • Energy, energy tanks, and continue tokens
  • Audio settings (music and sound volume)

This data stays on your device unless you clear the App’s data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove it through your device settings. We do not operate our own servers that receive this progress data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob (for example, to unlock a free continue). Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks, whether a reward was earned)

This information is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws. For details, see Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.

You can often limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, reset or opt out of the Advertising ID on Android) and through Google’s ad settings.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App may offer optional paid items (such as energy tank packs and continue packs) through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google. Google may process payment details, order information, and related account data according to Google’s terms and privacy policy. We receive confirmation of what was purchased so the App can unlock the corresponding items; we do not receive your full payment card number.

5. Children’s privacy

Wyvern Rush is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or related services, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Limited data may be processed by service providers that power the App’s features, currently including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play — distribution and in-app purchases

These providers act under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App.

7. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Advertising and purchase records retained by Google are governed by Google’s retention practices.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to a mobile game that primarily stores data on-device. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. International users

If you use the App from outside the country where the developer is based, your information may be processed in other countries by Google and its partners, including the United States, subject to their safeguards and applicable law (including GDPR/CCPA rights where they apply).

10. Your choices

  • Uninstall the App or clear App storage to remove local progress data.
  • Adjust device ad / privacy settings to limit personalized ads.
  • Manage subscriptions and purchases through Google Play.
  • Contact us (email above) with privacy questions or requests.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
